---
title: Add an RSS feed
description: Import posts from another site into the Lite Site on a schedule.
---

By the end of this page, one RSS feed will be importing into the Lite Site and you will know where its entries appear.

This step is optional. A feed brings in posts from another site, such as a county alerts page. Each new item is published on the Lite Site as an RSS entry. Entries also have a page on the main site; see [RSS entries](/docs/guides/rss-entries/).

## Set up the feed

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the RSS screen

In the dashboard, go to **Lite Site > RSS**. Until you add a feed, the screen says **No RSS feeds yet**.

<Screenshot label="The RSS screen with no feeds yet, the Add RSS Feed button picked out" src="/screenshots/getting-started/add-an-rss-feed-1.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Select Add RSS Feed

Select **Add RSS Feed**. A drawer opens.

</Step>
<Step>

### Fill in the drawer

Enter the **RSS Feed URL**. Choose a **Frequency**, such as **Every 10 Minutes**. Choose an **Author**. Imported entries are published under this author.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add the feed

Select **Add RSS Feed** at the bottom of the drawer.

<Screenshot label="The Add RSS Feed drawer filled in, with its Add RSS Feed button picked out" src="/screenshots/getting-started/add-an-rss-feed-2.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the feed

Two tabs appear, **Entries** and **Feeds**. The feed is listed in **Feeds**, with its frequency and author.

<Screenshot label="The Feeds tab listing four feeds, the county alerts feed picked out" src="/screenshots/getting-started/add-an-rss-feed-3.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### See the entries

Open the **Entries** tab. After the feed's first import, it lists the entries the feed brought in. They also appear on the Lite Site homepage.

<Screenshot label="The Entries tab listing entries imported from the feeds" src="/screenshots/getting-started/add-an-rss-feed-4.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Need it quicker?">
The shortest frequency is **Every 5 Minutes**. Feeds import on a schedule, so a new item can take that long to appear.
</Callout>
