---
title: Turn on Lite Site
description: Switch Lite Site on and open it as a reader would.
---

By the end of this page, Lite Site will be on and you will have seen its homepage.

Lite Site is off after you install the plugin. Until you turn it on, its address is an ordinary address on your site: readers who visit it see the site's "page not found" page.

## Turn it on

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the settings

In the dashboard, go to **Lite Site > Settings**. The **General** tab opens.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the box

On a site with a paywall or membership plugin, restricted articles can show in full on the Lite Site as soon as it is on. Before you turn it on, set **Excluded Categories** or **Excluded Tags** for restricted content. After you turn it on, open a restricted article to confirm. See [the FAQ answer](/docs/guides/faq/#does-it-work-with-membership-translation-or-redirect-plugins).

Check **Enable Lite Site**.

<Screenshot label="Lite Site settings with Enable Lite Site checked, picked out" src="/screenshots/getting-started/turn-on-lite-site-1.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Save

Select **Save**.

<Screenshot label="Lite Site settings with the Save button picked out" src="/screenshots/getting-started/turn-on-lite-site-2.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Open the Lite Site

Select **View Lite Site**, next to the **Lite Site Path** field. It opens in a new tab. The button works once Lite Site is saved as on.

<Screenshot label="Lite Site settings after saving, with the View Lite Site button picked out" src="/screenshots/getting-started/turn-on-lite-site-3.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the homepage

The Lite Site homepage lists the newest posts, with the page links at the bottom. This is what a signed-out reader sees.

<Screenshot label="The Lite Site homepage at /lite/, as a signed-out reader sees it" src="/screenshots/getting-started/turn-on-lite-site-4.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Help readers find it">
The address is the site's address followed by `/lite/`. You can change the path in the **Lite Site Path** field. Link to it from the main site's header or footer, so readers find it before they need it.
</Callout>
