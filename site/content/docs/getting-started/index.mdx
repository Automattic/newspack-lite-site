By the end of this page, you will know what Lite Site does and what the next three pages cover.

Lite Site is a stripped-down copy of a news site, made to load on a weak or expensive connection. It shows headlines and text with no theme and no ads. Images wait until a reader taps to load them. Lite Site is part of Emergency Mode for News.

Readers find it at an address on the main site, `/lite/` by default. Posts, pages and the theme do not change, and search engines are told to list only the posts and pages of the main site. Imported RSS entries also have a page on your main site, which shows in your site's own search. Search engines are asked not to list these pages. See [RSS entries](/docs/guides/rss-entries/).

## Words used in these docs

- **Lite Site**: The stripped-down version of the site, at its own address.
- **Lite Site homepage**: A newest-first list of posts and RSS entries.
- **RSS feed**: A feed from another site, imported on a schedule.
- **RSS entry**: One item imported from an RSS feed. It is published on the Lite Site.

## Who can do what

Turning on Lite Site and changing its settings requires an Administrator. Adding RSS feeds also requires an Administrator. Anyone who can open the **Entries** tab sees every published entry. Editors and Administrators can edit and remove any entry, and Authors only their own. Imported entries belong to the feed's author, who must be able to publish.

## Set it up

1. [Install the plugin](/docs/getting-started/install-the-plugin/).
2. [Turn on Lite Site](/docs/getting-started/turn-on-lite-site/).
3. [Add an RSS feed](/docs/getting-started/add-an-rss-feed/), if you want one. This step is optional.
