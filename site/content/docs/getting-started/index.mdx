By the end of this page, you will know what Lite Site does and what the next three pages cover.

Lite Site is a stripped-down copy of a news site, made to load on a weak or expensive connection. It shows headlines and text with no scripts, no ads and no theme styling. Images wait until a reader taps to load them. Lite Site is part of Emergency Mode for News.

Readers find it at an address on the main site, `/lite/` by default. Nothing else on the site changes, and search engines only index the main site.

## Words used in these docs

- **Lite Site**: The stripped-down version of the site, at its own address.
- **Lite Site homepage**: A newest-first list of posts and RSS entries.
- **RSS feed**: A feed from another site, imported on a schedule.
- **RSS entry**: One item imported from an RSS feed. It is published on the Lite Site.

## Who can do what

Turning on Lite Site and changing its settings requires an Administrator. Adding RSS feeds also requires an Administrator. People who can edit posts can see the RSS entries.

## Set it up

1. [Install the plugin](/docs/getting-started/install-the-plugin/).
2. [Turn on Lite Site](/docs/getting-started/turn-on-lite-site/).
3. [Add an RSS feed](/docs/getting-started/add-an-rss-feed/), if you want one. This step is optional.
