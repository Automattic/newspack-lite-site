By the end of this page, you will know how a post is simplified on the Lite Site, what readers can do with images and links, and how to find the full version.

Each Lite Site article is the post's text on a plain page. It shows the title, the byline and the date, then the text. Posts by more than one author show every author. Readers see the version a signed-out visitor sees.

## Navigation

<Steps>
<Step>

### Find the links at the top

Two links sit above the title. **← Back to Lite Site homepage** returns to the list. **View full version** opens the same article on the main site.

</Step>
</Steps>

## Images

Images are not loaded until the reader asks for them. This keeps pages small on a slow connection.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Read the placeholder

Where an image was, the reader sees a box. It shows **Image:** followed by the image's alt text, and **Caption:** followed by the caption, including any photo credit written into it. Write alt text for every image, so the box tells readers what they would see.

<Screenshot label="An image placeholder with its alt text, caption and Load image button, picked out" src="/screenshots/guides/lite-articles-2.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Load the image

The reader selects **Load image**. The photo replaces the box, with its caption.

<Screenshot label="The same article after Load image, showing the photo with its caption and credit" src="/screenshots/guides/lite-articles-3.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
</Steps>

## Text and links

The Lite Site keeps headings, paragraphs, lists, quotes, links, bold and italic text. Other blocks keep only their text and links: columns lose their layout, buttons become plain links, and an embed from another site shows as its web address. Scripts are removed.

<Steps>
<Step>

### Spot an external link

A link to another site ends with an arrow, so readers know it leaves the Lite Site. If **Open external links in a new tab** is checked in [Settings](/docs/guides/settings/), these links open in a new tab.

<Screenshot label="An external link marked with an arrow, picked out" src="/screenshots/guides/lite-articles-4.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Check the footer

The footer you set in **Footer HTML** shows at the bottom of every article.

<Screenshot label="The footer at the bottom of a Lite Site article, picked out" src="/screenshots/guides/lite-articles-5.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
</Steps>

## The full version

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the full version

**View full version** opens the article on the main site, with its theme, images and layout. Search engines index only this version.

<Screenshot label="The full version of the same article on the main site, in the Newspack theme" src="/screenshots/guides/lite-articles-6.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
</Steps>

## View the Lite Site from the dashboard

<Steps>
<Step>

### Use the toolbar link

While Lite Site is on, a **View Lite Site** link shows in the toolbar on the Lite Site screens. It opens the Lite Site in a new tab.

<Screenshot label="The View Lite Site link in the toolbar on the Lite Site Settings screen, picked out" src="/screenshots/guides/lite-articles-7.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info" title="Changes can take a few minutes">
Article pages are kept for 15 minutes. Saving a post clears its stored Lite Site article, so the change usually shows within a few minutes. Saving settings, such as a new footer, can take up to 15 minutes to show on an article that was recently opened.
</Callout>

<Callout title="Live coverage">
A live coverage page keeps its updates on the Lite Site, shown as text, each with the time it was posted. [Rolling Coverage](https://rolling-coverage.space.fast/) adds its feed to these pages.
</Callout>
