By the end of this page, you will know how to find an imported entry, view it on the Lite Site and at its source, edit it, and remove it.

An RSS entry is one item a feed imported. Entries are listed on the **Entries** tab of **Lite Site > RSS**, and on the Lite Site homepage with your posts. Editors and Administrators see all entries. Authors and Contributors see only their own, and entries belong to the feed's author. Before the first import it says **Entries appear here after the first import.**

Each entry also has its own page on the main site, and entries show in the main site's search results.

## Find an entry

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the Entries tab

Go to **Lite Site > RSS** and select **Entries**.

</Step>
<Step>

### Read the list

Each row has a **Title**, with its **Feed** under it, then **Author**, **Categories**, **Tags**, **Status** and **Date**. Status is one of **Published**, **Draft**, **Pending**, **Scheduled**, **Private** or **Trash**.

<Screenshot label="The newest entry, with its feed under the title, picked out" src="/screenshots/guides/rss-entries-1.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
</Steps>

## Open an entry

<Steps>
<Step>

### Open the actions menu

Select the actions menu on the row.

</Step>
<Step>

### Choose an action

**View on Lite Site** opens the entry as readers see it. **View Original** opens the page the feed took it from. **Edit** opens the entry in the editor.

<Screenshot label="The actions menu of an entry, with View on Lite Site and View Original" src="/screenshots/guides/rss-entries-2.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### See it on the Lite Site

This is the entry on the Lite Site, as a signed-out reader sees it.

<Screenshot label="An imported entry on the Lite Site, as a signed-out reader sees it" src="/screenshots/guides/rss-entries-3.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
</Steps>

## Remove entries

<Steps>
<Step>

### Move to the trash

Choose **Move to Trash** from the actions menu. A message confirms it and offers **Undo**. You can select several entries and trash them together.

</Step>
<Step>

### Restore or delete

A trashed entry shows the **Trash** status. Choose **Restore** to bring an entry back, or **Delete Permanently** to remove it. Permanent deletion asks you to confirm, and it can't be undone.

To keep an unwanted item off the Lite Site while its source still lists it, set the entry to **Draft**. Drafts never show on the Lite Site. You can also pause or delete the feed first. **Delete Permanently** brings the item back on the next import, and WordPress empties the trash after 30 days, so a trashed entry is only held for that long.

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Add an entry by hand">
**Add RSS Entry**, at the top right once there are entries, opens the editor for a new entry.
</Callout>
