title: The Lite Site homepage
description: What readers see on the Lite Site homepage and how they reach older posts.
---

By the end of this page, you will know what the Lite Site homepage shows, in what order, and how readers move through it.

The homepage is at the Lite Site address, `/lite/` by default. It shows the site name, then a list of post titles, newest first. RSS entries from your feeds are listed with the posts. It shows what a signed-out reader sees.

## What the homepage shows

<Steps>
<Step>

### Sticky posts stand out

A sticky post is shown in bold, so readers notice it. Make a post sticky in the editor, as you would on the main site.

<Screenshot label="The Lite Site homepage with the sticky post, in bold, picked out" src="/screenshots/guides/lite-homepage-1.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Readers page through older posts

Page links at the bottom let readers move to older posts. How many posts each page lists is set by **Posts per Page** in [Settings](/docs/guides/settings/). The footer you wrote in **Footer HTML** shows below the page links.

<Screenshot label="The page links at the bottom of the Lite Site homepage, picked out" src="/screenshots/guides/lite-homepage-2.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
<Step>

### Readers can go to the full site

**View full site**, at the top left, takes readers to the main site.

<Screenshot label="The View full site link at the top of the Lite Site homepage, picked out" src="/screenshots/guides/lite-homepage-3.webp" width={3024} height={1964} />

</Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Choose what is listed">
To change which posts appear, use the **Content** filters in [Settings](/docs/guides/settings/). The list holds posts and RSS entries. Pages are not listed here, though each page has its own Lite Site address.
</Callout>
